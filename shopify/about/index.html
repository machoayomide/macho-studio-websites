ABOUT

I build stores the way a tailor cuts a suit.

Before I wrote code, I spent two years as a fashion design apprentice. A good suit is cut for one body. A good store is built for one brand. I measure first, then cut.

HOW I STARTED

The small jobs taught me where stores break.

I started freelancing in 2021. My first Shopify jobs were simple: setting up stores, uploading products and helping owners keep orders and stock in line.

Those jobs taught me that a store's hardest problems are often behind the homepage: stock that does not match, an order nobody forwarded, or a migration that stopped halfway.

Since then, I've worked on ecommerce migrations, inventory connections and storefront builds. I also build my own business platforms, independently of client projects. Operating products of my own keeps me close to the everyday work of maintaining software.

HOW I WORK

The rules I follow.

Draft theme first. I don't change your live theme without a safe review path.

Ask before adding apps. Every app has an ongoing cost or maintenance burden.

Write decisions down. Scope, milestones and handover notes belong in writing.

Tell you early. Plan restrictions and unsupported features are discussed before payment.
