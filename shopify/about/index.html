ABOUT

I build stores the way a tailor cuts a suit.

Before I became a developer, I spent two years learning fashion design. Every suit is made to fit someone. I believe every store should fit its brand too.

HOW I STARTED

The small jobs taught me where stores break.

I started freelancing in 2021. My first Shopify jobs were store setup, adding products, and keeping stock and orders in order.

I learned that many store problems happen behind the scenes: wrong stock numbers, missing orders, or store moves that fail.

Now I build stores, move stores, and connect inventory systems. I also build my own business tools, so I know what it takes to keep software working.

HOW I WORK

The rules I follow.

Draft theme first. I don't change your live theme without a safe review path.

Ask before adding apps. Every app has an ongoing cost or maintenance burden.

Write decisions down. Scope, milestones and handover notes belong in writing.

Tell you early. Plan restrictions and unsupported features are discussed before payment.
