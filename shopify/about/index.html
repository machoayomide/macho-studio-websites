THE APPROACH

The details are the work.

I started with simple storefront projects and moved toward harder challenges: migrations, integrations, wholesale purchasing and custom experiences.

I don’t think a business needs new code for the sake of it. First I look at the goals and the current setup. Sometimes a small theme fix is the right answer. Sometimes there is a system behind the store that needs to change.

My work is practical: scope clearly, build in safe checkpoints, test with real examples, then explain the result in plain English.

Macho / Shopify is one specialist practice within Macho Studio. It focuses on ecommerce while the wider studio also works on apps, websites and connected systems.
