WHAT CHANGED

The store, layer by layer.

01 · FRONT

What shoppers see

An editorial storefront with licensed brand typography and a short product finder based on hold and finish.

02 · MIDDLE

How buying works

Separate retail and professional buying journeys, with trade catalogs shown only to eligible approved buyers.

03 · BACK

The operations

Applications and launch-control settings help the owner approve trade customers and publish content when ready.

A single store structure for retail and trade, pending launch and owner approvals.
