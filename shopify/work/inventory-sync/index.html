01 / THE CONTEXT

The brief

An ecommerce business had product stock spread across several sales channels, increasing the risk of mismatched inventory and manual updates.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

What needed solving

Inventory data and product codes needed to match across systems with different listing rules and variant structures.

03 / THE SOLUTION

How I approached it

Zoho Inventory was used as the central record. I configured integrations and added a Deluge script to generate SKUs in a consistent category pattern.
