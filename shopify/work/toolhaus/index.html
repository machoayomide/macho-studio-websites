01 / THE CONTEXT

The brief

I kept seeing similar ecommerce problems: clumsy storefronts, heavy themes and repetitive setup work. ToolHaus became a place to turn those lessons into reusable growth kits and storefront services.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

What needed solving

Build an editable storefront without relying on a heavy frontend framework or loading scripts on pages that do not need them.

03 / THE SOLUTION

How I approached it

I planned page templates, reusable snippets and light client-side behavior, keeping the storefront maintainable through Shopify’s normal editor.
