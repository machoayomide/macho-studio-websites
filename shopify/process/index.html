QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Do you need access to my live store?+

For most projects, I begin by reviewing the existing setup and working in a draft theme or safe environment. We agree access before changes.

Can you finish a migration someone else started?+

Yes. I begin by checking what already moved, then plan a safe completion without unnecessary duplicates.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?+

The goal is to use editable Shopify sections, blocks and metafields where appropriate, with a clear handover.

Can you connect my store to Zoho or another system?+

I have experience with Shopify, Zoho Inventory and Amazon. The exact integration depends on your data and supported APIs.

Can you help with B2B without Shopify Plus?+

Some native B2B functionality is available on lower Shopify plans, but features and catalog limits vary. I verify your plan first.

How do you price projects?+

I usually start with a clear scope and milestone-based pricing, so requirements, deliverables and review points are defined.
