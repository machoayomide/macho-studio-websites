LEGAL / PRIVACY

PRIVACY
IN PLAIN WORDS.

A clear explanation of Store Check answers, optional activity events and contact preferences.

About this site

MACHO operates this independent Shopify development website. If you have privacy questions, contact hello@macho.studio.

Your Store Check answers

The Store Check uses the answers you enter in your own browser to generate a preliminary report. We do not automatically transmit your sales estimate, traffic estimate, store URL, or form responses to our activity feed. When you choose to proceed to an enquiry, your report may temporarily be transferred through your browser's session storage to prepare the message. You must explicitly select whether to include it in the enquiry. Session storage is ordinarily cleared when that browser session ends.

Optional follow-up and newsletter

You can use the Store Check without giving an email. If you click Save my email, we save your email and your request for a store follow-up. Your quiz answers are shared only if you tick Include my store-check report. Newsletter permission is a separate, unticked choice. We never add store-review contacts to the newsletter unless they ask.

When you subscribe, we save your email, consent wording, signup time, and source in a private Cloudflare database. MACHO uses these details to follow up or send the newsletter you requested. They are kept separately from anonymous activity events. We keep follow-up requests for up to 12 months and newsletter records until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. A minimal unsubscribe record prevents further newsletter use. The owner can access and export these records only with an admin key.

You can unsubscribe using your personal unsubscribe link in a newsletter, or email hello@macho.studio with the subject Unsubscribe. You can also ask us to correct or remove your saved details. We do not sell your email or send your quiz report to visitor-activity alerts.

Demo requests

When you request a demo, we save your name, email, selected service, optional store link, and message for up to 12 months so MACHO can follow up. These requests are private and kept apart from activity alerts. A demo request does not subscribe you to the newsletter unless you choose that option.

Optional activity alerts

We ask permission before enabling our own visitor-activity feature. If you agree, the website sends anonymous event information such as pages viewed, approximate visit length, scroll progress, Store Check steps started/completed and clicks to WhatsApp or email. These events use a randomly generated session identifier, a short visit identifier, device category, an approximate country provided by our hosting platform, the page path without its query string, and the event time. We do not include typed names, email addresses, phone numbers, full referral URLs, or your answers about store revenue.

If you allow activity tracking, we remember your choice and a pseudonymous marker for recognizing repeat visits in your browser. We keep recent event records in a protected Cloudflare KV store, when configured, for up to seven days; alerts may be sent to the site's owner through Telegram, if configured. Activity messages are not proof that someone became a customer. Declining tracking will not prevent you from using the website or Store Check. Select Activity preferences at the page footer to reconsider your choice.

Hosting and third-party services

Cloudflare serves the website and may process standard network and security logs. Some animation assets can be delivered by CDN providers. WhatsApp and email services apply their own privacy policies. Please avoid sharing financial statements or customer records through public forms or links.

Contact and project enquiries

The contact form currently prepares a message rather than sending it automatically. You choose when to open WhatsApp or your email program. Those services handle any contact details and message content you choose to send. No website visitor-activity record contains the body of that message.

Data questions

You can ask how your information is used by emailing hello@macho.studio. Contact us if you want to see, change, or remove your saved details.
