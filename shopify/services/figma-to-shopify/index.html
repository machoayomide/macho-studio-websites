QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

What do you need from Figma?

Desktop and mobile frames, fonts, colors and assets. View-only access is enough to review.

What if I do not have mobile designs?

I can propose mobile layouts in the same design language before starting development.

Can you work with my designer?

Yes. We can agree the handoff steps and communication arrangement in the project scope.
