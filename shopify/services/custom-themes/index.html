QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Do you build from scratch or on a base theme?

Usually on a solid base such as Horizon. I build from scratch when the design requires something a base cannot do.

Can my team edit the finished pages?

Yes. Editable sections, text, images and section order stay in Shopify's theme editor.

Do I need professional photos first?

No. Clear placeholders can keep design work moving, but your real product content is needed before launch.
