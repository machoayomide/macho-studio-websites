QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Will customer passwords move?

Passwords generally cannot be exported between commerce platforms. Customers may need to reset passwords in the new store.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

Correct redirects and preserved content reduce risk, but search ranking changes cannot be guaranteed.

Who pays for migration software?

Any paid migration tool is quoted before use and charged separately from development.
