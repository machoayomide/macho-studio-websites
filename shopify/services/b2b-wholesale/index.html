QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Not always. Basic, Grow and Advanced include many B2B features, but direct company catalogs and other advanced features have plan limits. I check before quoting.

Can wholesale and retail share one store?

Yes, for many workflows. Whether it is best depends on your pricing and operations.

Can shoppers see wholesale prices?

Trade access and pricing should be configured so approved customers see the intended catalog. We test that before launch.
