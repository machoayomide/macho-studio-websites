QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Not always. Some B2B features depend on your Shopify plan. I will check before giving a price.

Can wholesale and retail share one store?

Yes, for many setups. We will check if it fits your prices and how you sell.

Can shoppers see wholesale prices?

Approved B2B customers should see the right prices and catalog. We test this before launch.
