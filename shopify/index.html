INSIDE YOUR STORE

Every store has three layers.

The shop people see is only the start. Your checkout rules and the systems behind each order need to work just as well.

01 · FRONT

The theme.

The pages, photos and buttons shoppers see. This is where trust is won or lost in a few seconds.

02 · MIDDLE

How people buy.

Apps, discounts and checkout. The rules that decide what a shopper pays and how they pay.

03 · BACK

What keeps it running.

Stock, orders and data. The numbers that must match your warehouse, marketplace and books.

Most developers only touch the front. I work on all three, so a fix in one layer does not break another.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before we start.

Will my store go offline while you work?

No. I build on a draft copy of your theme. Your live store keeps selling until you approve the switch.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Most storefront work runs on other plans. Some custom Shopify Functions and certain advanced B2B features require Plus. I check the plan before proposing anything.

Can my team edit the store after you finish?

Yes. I build editable theme sections and provide handover notes.
