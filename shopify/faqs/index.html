FAQS

Questions store owners ask me.

Simple answers. If you still have questions, ask me.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you decide.

Before we start

How much does a Shopify project cost?

The price depends on the job. We will agree on the work and price before I start.

How long does it take?

A small fix may take a few days. A full store may take weeks. We will agree on dates.

What do you need from me?

Share your store link, brand files if you have them, and the problem.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes. I work with clients around the world and keep plans in writing.

During the build

Will my live store go offline?

Not for normal theme work. I use a draft theme and publish after you approve it. Migrations need a separate plan.

Do I need to share my password?

No. Give me Shopify access that you can remove later.

How do I see progress?

I share preview links and updates while I work.

What if I want additional features?

We can discuss small changes. New features need a new price.

After launch

Who owns the custom theme work?

We will write down who owns the finished work and any third-party licenses.

Can my team make changes?

Yes, I can include editable theme sections and simple handover notes.

What if something breaks?

I fix bugs covered by our agreement. Extra work has a separate price.

About Shopify

Do I need Shopify Plus?

Many storefront and B2B tasks work on lower plans. Custom apps containing Shopify Function APIs require Plus, and some other features have restrictions.

Should we use an app or custom code?

If an app fits, I may suggest it. I use custom code when you need special features.

Will migration affect Google rankings?

Redirects and content checks help protect SEO, but search rankings can still change.

My Shopify Scripts stopped working. Can you help?

Yes. Shopify Scripts were deactivated on June 30, 2026. We can map the old rules to supported alternatives.

Are you part of Shopify?

No. MACHO is an independent developer brand and is not owned or operated by Shopify.
