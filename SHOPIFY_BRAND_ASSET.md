# Shopify shopping bag

`shopify/assets/shopify-shopping-bag.svg` is the unmodified full-colour Shopping Bag supplied on Shopify’s official brand-assets page:
https://www.shopify.com/brand-assets

Downloaded from:
https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/brochure/assets/brand-assets/shopify-logo-shopping-bag-full-color-66166b2e55d67988b56b4bd28b63c271e2b9713358cb723070a92bde17ad7d63.svg

The hero links the asset to Shopify, preserves its proportions and colours, and identifies MACHO as independent. Decorative motion belongs to the surrounding experience. The former generic NoLagHere / Sketchfab bag is no longer embedded or displayed.
