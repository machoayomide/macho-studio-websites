# MACHO website writing style

Write all new customer-facing text in clear English that a Grade 6 reader can understand.

- Use common words and short sentences. Aim for one idea in each sentence.
- Speak directly to the visitor: "you", "your store", "I will", and "we".
- Explain what the work does and how it helps. Do not use fancy sales phrases.
- Keep technical terms where accuracy needs them. Examples: Shopify, Liquid, Flutter, React Native, Wix Studio, Velo, CMS, CRM, API, webhook, SEO, B2B, Shopify Functions, checkout, SKU, Figma, Zoho Inventory, Cloudflare, Resend, and JavaScript.
- Do not change project facts, prices, dates, plan requirements, confirmed results, legal/trademark notes, or service guarantees. Do not invent results.
- Keep testimonials and direct client quotes exactly as supplied. Do not simplify someone else's quote.
- Labels, buttons, error messages, forms, popups, Store Check hints, email confirmations, About, FAQs, privacy information, case studies, and mobile text follow this style.
- For privacy/legal text, keep every important disclosure and avoid changing what data is collected, where it goes, or how long it stays.
- Do not change technical names in code, CSS classes, attributes, API fields, file paths, or URLs just to make them easier to read.

## Examples

Before: "A connected workflow between Amazon orders and EWE warehouse fulfillment."
After: "A system that sends Amazon orders to EWE for warehouse fulfillment."

Before: "Your answers indicate a possible gap between visits and orders."
After: "Your answers may show that people visit but do not buy."

Before: "The enquiry could not be sent. Your details remain in the form."
After: "We could not send your message. Your details are still in the form."
