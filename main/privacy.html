THE HUMAN BEHIND THE CODE

Privacy and enquiries.

How MACHO uses your information when you contact us.

Who runs this website?

MACHO is an independent development brand operated by Muhammed Olalekan. Reach us at hello@macho.studio with privacy questions.

Optional website activity alerts

We ask before tracking how people use this website. If you agree, we track pages you visit, time on the site, how far you scroll, and contact buttons you click. We use random session and view IDs, your device type, a country estimate from Cloudflare, and the page path without its search details. We do not put your IP address, name, phone number, or form message in these activity alerts.

Your browser saves your choice and a marker for return visits. You can change your choice using Activity preferences in the footer. You can still use MACHO if you say no.

Event storage and notifications

When the Cloudflare activity service is on, it may save anonymous activity in Cloudflare KV for up to seven days. It may also send these alerts to MACHO through Telegram. These alerts do not prove that someone bought a product or sent a project enquiry.

Contact enquiries

When you send the contact form, your name, email, service, timeline, budget (if you add one), and project details go to our server and email provider. We use them to reply. These details are not put in the anonymous activity alerts. If sending fails, your message stays in the form so you can try again or choose WhatsApp. Opening an email app does not mean a message was sent.

To help stop spam, Cloudflare may keep a count of requests for one hour using an ID made from the IP address. This count does not include your name, email, or message. Contact us if you want to correct or delete a message you sent.

Hosting and external services

Cloudflare may collect basic network and security logs. Some site features may use outside services. Those services have their own privacy rules.

Your privacy questions

To see, fix, or delete your messages or activity records, email hello@macho.studio.
