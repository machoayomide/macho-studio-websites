THE HUMAN BEHIND THE CODE

Privacy and enquiries.

How your enquiry data is used when you contact MACHO.

Who runs this website?

MACHO is an independent development brand operated by Muhammed Olalekan. Reach us at machomanexpert@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Optional visitor activity notifications

We ask permission before collecting anonymous visitor activity such as which pages were viewed, visit duration, broad scroll milestones and clicks to contact methods. If you agree, events use a random browser session identifier, short view identifier, approximate country supplied by our hosting platform, device category and page path (without search query). No IP address, form contents, phone number or contact name is included in the visitor alert feed.

Your consent preference and a marker for return visits are remembered in your browser. You can change your choice with the Activity preferences control in the footer. Declining does not affect access to MACHO.

Event storage and notifications

With the Cloudflare activity service enabled, recent anonymous events may be stored in a protected Cloudflare KV binding for up to seven days and sent to MACHO's owner through Telegram. These are activity indicators, not proof of purchases or confirmed enquiries.

Contact enquiries

On our static website, project details are prepared in your browser until you choose to send them using WhatsApp or email. Those platforms handle the information you deliberately send in line with their privacy policies. We do not transmit completed form contents through activity alerts.

Hosting and external services

Cloudflare may process standard network and security logs. Our pages may use external hosting for scripts and interactive assets; other sites have their own privacy rules.

Your privacy questions

For access, correction or deletion questions about messages or event records, email machomanexpert@gmail.com.
